WHAT YOU'LL FIND HERE

Built for a slower kind of survival

Aevon SMP's goal is simple: give every player the best experience the skies have to offer. Every journey and every adventure here is meant to matter — full of good vibes, new friends, and memories worth keeping. Whoever you are, whenever you join, Aevon SMP welcomes you with open arms and an adventure already waiting.

🗝️

Dungeons

Defeat bosses across 5 unique dungeons — Forest, Molten Ruins, The Fallen Kingdom, Void, and Cave of Flayed Remains — for exchangeable drops, premium items, and rare dungeon-specific keys. Each dungeon hides its own crates, Spirit, and Merchant, and every mob you clear pays out in-game cash.

📈

Grindable Ranks

Rank up to unlock access to higher-tier dungeons and new opportunities across the server, earning rewards with every rank.

💎

APoints & ACoins

Two premium currencies of equal importance. Earn APoints in-game just by playing — AFK Pond, Piñata, voting, and more — or grab ACoins by topping up through a Discord ticket.

🎨

Cosmetics

New cosmetics drop every 15 days, and they're more than just looks — each one adds real stats to help your character survive tougher bosses. Browse them at spawn or in Discord.

💰

Economy

A player-driven economy at the core of progression — earn cash easily, convert it to APoints, rank up, shop, or open your own player shop or warp for others to visit.

⚔️

Controlled PVP

PVP is on but kept fair through /league — you can only fight players in your own league. The PVP world has zero restrictions, while the Nether, The End, and Resource World allow PVP with limitations.

🏡

Land Claims

Grief prevention built in — claim your base before you build. Staff aren't liable for unclaimed losses, so grab extra claim blocks anytime at /ashop.

🛡️

Team / Clan

Work together, earn EXP, increase Team Power and Dominance, complete quests, build alliances, defeat rivals, and climb to the top!

SERVER NEWS & UPDATES

ATeam — Team Management System

Build your team, grow stronger together, and compete for dominance across AevonSMP.

NEW FEATURE

ATeam is a complete team/clan system that lets players create teams, recruit members, level up together, complete quests, form alliances, fight rivals, and compete to become one of the strongest teams on the server.

Main Features

Team Management — Create, invite, kick, leave, disband, and manage members.

Team Progression — Earn shared Team EXP and level up from Level 1–10.

Team Power — Measures overall team strength with a power cap based on Team Level.

PvP & Dominance — Player kills earn Team EXP and 5 Dominance Points per kill.

Team Quests — Contribute resources, upgrade quests, and unlock Frenzy bonuses.

Alliances & Enemies — Ally with other teams or declare rivals.

Join Requests — Players can apply to teams; Captains can Accept or Deny applications.

Team Home & Warps — Set locations for members to quickly travel together.

Team Bank & Wealth — Manage team funds and wealth progression.

Inactivity Protection — Teams with no active members for 30 days are automatically disbanded.
