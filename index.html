WHAT YOU'LL FIND HERE

Built for a slower kind of survival

Aevon SMP's goal is simple: give every player the best experience the skies have to offer. Every journey and every adventure here is meant to matter — full of good vibes, new friends, and memories worth keeping. Whoever you are, whenever you join, Aevon SMP welcomes you with open arms and an adventure already waiting.

🗝️

Dungeons

Defeat bosses across 5 unique dungeons — Forest, Molten Ruins, The Fallen Kingdom, Void, and Cave of Flayed Remains — for exchangeable drops, premium items, and rare dungeon-specific keys. Each dungeon hides its own crates, Spirit, and Merchant, and every mob you clear pays out in-game cash.

📈

Grindable Ranks

Rank up to unlock access to higher-tier dungeons and new opportunities across the server, earning rewards with every rank.

💎

APoints & ACoins

Two premium currencies of equal importance. Earn APoints in-game just by playing — AFK Pond, Piñata, voting, and more — or grab ACoins by topping up through a Discord ticket.

🎨

Cosmetics

New cosmetics drop every 15 days, and they're more than just looks — each one adds real stats to help your character survive tougher bosses. Browse them at spawn or in Discord.

💰

Economy

A player-driven economy at the core of progression — earn cash easily, convert it to APoints, rank up, shop, or open your own player shop or warp for others to visit.

⚔️

Controlled PVP

PVP is on but kept fair through /league — you can only fight players in your own league. The PVP world has zero restrictions, while the Nether, The End, and Resource World allow PVP with limitations.

🏡

Land Claims

Grief prevention built in — claim your base before you build. Staff aren't liable for unclaimed losses, so grab extra claim blocks anytime at /ashop.

PATCH NOTES

Server updates

AevonSMP Update v2.3 — new features, improvements & server optimizations.

NEW FEATURE

Order System — Need an Item?

Looking for a specific item but don't have time to gather it yourself? You can now create an Order Request and let other players fulfill it.

How it works:

  1. Hold the item you want to order.
  2. Type /aorder <Price> <Quantity>

Example — while holding an Apple: /aorder 100 5. This broadcasts your request to the entire server. Players who have the item can accept your order with /adeliver. Buying items from other players has never been easier!

NEW FEATURE

Spawner Crate — I Want That!

A new Spawner Crate is now available at spawn! Grab a Spawner Key at the Key Exchange, then head to /spawn to get yours.

NEW FEATURE

Chest Sorter — Organize Everything Instantly!

Tired of manually sorting your inventory and chests? Now you can sort your items automatically:

  • Shift + Click anywhere in your inventory to instantly organize your items.
  • Use /chestsort to configure and manage your Auto Sorter settings.

Keeping your storage clean has never been this easy.

IMPROVEMENT

AJobs Update — Fisherman Buff!

Fishing just became much more rewarding! The Fisherman job now earns more experience depending on the rarity of the item you catch. Time to grab your fishing rod and level up faster.

FIXED

Backpack Update — Fully Fixed!

Great news! The Backpack System has been completely fixed and is now running more reliably than ever.

To improve server performance and ensure a smoother gameplay experience, the backpack limit has been adjusted:

  • Previous limit: 3 backpacks per player
  • New limit: 1 backpack per player

This change helps reduce server load while maintaining a stable experience for everyone.

PERFORMANCE

Massive Server Optimization!

We've successfully optimized the server by approximately 30–50%, resulting in smoother gameplay and improved overall performance. As part of these optimizations, you may notice slight changes to certain mob behaviors — these adjustments are intentional and help keep the server running efficiently.

If you encounter any bugs or unexpected issues after the update, please don't hesitate to create a support ticket. Your feedback helps us continue improving AevonSMP.

Thank you for supporting AevonSMP! We hope you enjoy everything included in Update v2.3. Have fun, and happy grinding!
